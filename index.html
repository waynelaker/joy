JOY LAKER: A LIFE OF GRACE & SERVICE

Best Mum in the World Celebrates her Birthday Today; Husband Mike Claims he's "The Luckiest Man Alive."

The 1970 New Year's Romance

Born in the monumental year of 1953, Joy arrived as the world was witnessing history. While Queen Elizabeth II was being crowned and Sir Edmund Hillary was conquering Everest, a more significant legend was beginning her journey in Auckland. Growing up, Joy has always embodied the spirit of resilience and elegance that defines the best of New Zealand.

A pivotal moment occurred on January 1st, 1970. As a new decade dawned, Joy met her future husband, Mike Laker. What started as a chance New Year's meeting blossomed into a legendary Auckland romance spanning over fifty years. Together, they have built a life characterized by laughter and an unwavering commitment to family.

Friends describe Joy as the "engine room" of the family, always ensuring everyone is supported while keeping her own poise. Today, as she celebrates her birthday, her legacy as a mother and community pillars is brighter than ever.

The Rollerworld Legacy

For many years, Joy was a key figure at Rollerworld, a specialized company known for importing and supplying top-tier skating and boarding gear. Her expertise helped outfit countless Auckland enthusiasts with roller skates, skateboards, and inline skates.

Her technical knowledge of the industry made Rollerworld a trusted name under her watch. "She knew every bearing and board in the warehouse," says a former colleague. Her dedication to the business was matched only by her service to the local community.

Administrative Excellence

Since 1986, Joy has been the silent power behind the Counties Gymnastics Club. Her work as Secretary and Treasurer has been the administrative backbone of the club for nearly 40 years, ensuring thousands of young athletes had a place to grow.
